Money Making Tips

Simple rules that can save you millions.

📊 Watch the spread

Don't just look at an item's AH price. Compare what you can buy it for with what players are actually paying.

📦 Buy in bulk

Orders are useful for getting large quantities of resources without buying individual listings.

⚠️ Don't trust old prices

DonutSMP has an active and inflationary economy. Always check the current market before investing.

💵 Protect your capital

Don't put your entire balance into one flip. Keep money available for opportunities.

🔎 Find undervalued items

The best flips usually come from listings that are significantly below the current market price.

📈 Scale what works

Once you find a profitable method, measure its real profit and increase your investment gradually.
